## Step by step

1. **Drive to the address or the map pin.** Both put you on County Road 161. It
   is a dirt road — that is the right road, keep going.

2. **Your GPS will say you have arrived. Keep driving about 100 metres.** That is
   roughly 330 feet, about the length of a football field. There is no way in
   where the GPS stops you.

3. **Watch for the green house on your LEFT.** The green house sits right on the
   road. The range is directly behind it, behind the tree line.

4. **Turn LEFT onto the dirt drive, just past the green house.** If you keep
   going and reach the next dirt turnoff, you have passed us. Turn around and
   come back to the green house.

5. **Go through GATE 1.** If it was closed when you got there, close it behind you.

6. **Follow the drive. It bends LEFT.** Stay on the main road. Do not take any
   side tracks.

7. **The road curves away. DO NOT follow the curve.** This is the one place
   people get it wrong. The road bends off to the side. Ignore it and keep going
   straight.

8. **Go STRAIGHT through GATE 2.** The gate is right at the curve. Straight
   through it, not around.

9. **The house is straight ahead through the gate.** Park where you see other
   vehicles.

10. **The classroom is the first-floor entry door.**
