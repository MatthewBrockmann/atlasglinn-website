M11 Multibang Device - 7 Bang

Has a military style fly off lever assembly with a straight pin pull and safety clip release system.

It is designed with a 2 second delay incorporated within the base ejecting sound unit which is constructed from tough recyclable plastic. The delay/sound unit within the device is full waterproofed as an addition benefit for the end user. The body is constructed from an HDPE plastic which is both a strong and durable. The overall design and shape of the M11 ensures that it will fit modern assault vests and webbing equipment currently on today’s market.

The M11 is a multiple bang device that gives the operator an advantage when entering a situation where a target needs to be distracted into believing the assault has started from multiple entry points. There are seven rapid consecutive bangs with bright flashes. 

It’s always recommended to remain behind cover when deploying and using our products during any type of training, simulation and CQB environment.
