M14 Smoke Deployment Device 

The M14 has a new metal military style fly off lever and straight pin pull mechanism and a safety clip release system, producing a high-density output of rich bright color smoke for approximately 45 seconds and is available in all the colors listed below.

 

The M14 is designed as a dual vented device for quick output cover/screening smoke replicating a military composition. The M14 body is constructed from an HDPE plastic which is both a strong, durable and making it fully waterproof. 

 

The overall design and shape of the M14 was to ensure it fits in modern assault vests and webbing equipment currently on the market.  Images may vary from actual product.
