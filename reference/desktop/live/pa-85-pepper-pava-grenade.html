This PA-85 Pepper PAVA Grenade is a non explosive device which carries a PAVA powder composition being actuated by a Co2 cartridge. The device will burst and disperse a 15 foot radius cloud of irritant PAVA powder.

**This product is a NON-HAZMAT item and CANNOT be shipped with HAZMAT items. Per the DOT a separate order will be required.**

This device is a non-flammable formulation designed for law enforcement officers and personal defense to quickly disable an aggressor and/or small group of non-compliant subjects.  When you're making the decision to purchase pepper spray or pepper device for yourself, it's important to familiarize yourself with pepper spray laws. While pepper spray is legal in all 50 states to carry and use for personal protection, some states and localities have specific regulations and restrictions.

Designed Specifically for Wildlife Deterrent & Personal Defense

DANGER: Intended to cause varying degrees of pain and injury, shortness of breath, coughing, tearing and burning sensation; which are temporary. Emits a cloud of irritant PAVA powder. Handle, store and use with extreme care and caution. Use ONLY as instructed.

INSTRUCTIONS: Use protective gear: gloves and eye protection. Hold the device with the fly off lever in the palm of your hand. Twist the triple safety pin left, unhook the safety latch, twist to the right, pull and throw.

CAUTION: For outdoor use ONLY. In rare circumstances, if used incorrectly, this product may cause damage to property or serious bodily injury. Be aware of wind direction when deploying this device. Keep out of reach from children.

STORAGE: Keep the device in a closed container, stored in a cool and dry place.

Specifications

Product Dimension: 6 inches High x 2 inch Diameter 

Net Weight: 7 ounces

Range: 15 Feet cloud of irritant

Contains: PAVA Powder
