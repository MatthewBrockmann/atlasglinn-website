# Marketing skills (vendored)

The `mkt-*` skills here are the marketing frameworks from [coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills)
(MIT), vendored 2026-09-06 as project-scope skills so every session on this repo loads them, the way the SEO toolchain is.
Copied from the brain repo's vetted pack (`00-tools/skills/marketingskills`, vetted 2026-07-01 by `install-vetted-skills.sh`:
no network, no keys). Names carry the `mkt-` prefix so they never collide with the `seo-*` pack (the pack's own seo-audit,
schema-markup, programmatic-seo, ai-seo, competitor-alternatives and site-architecture are left out for that reason; the
app-store, image and video generators are left out because they need vendor keys the rules do not allow in this repo).

Use: `/mkt-email-sequence`, `/mkt-revops`, `/mkt-lead-magnets`, `/mkt-referral-program`, `/mkt-pricing-strategy` … — the
frontmatter of each SKILL.md says when it applies. Marketing context for MAST and Atlas Glinn: `mast-backend/DATA-AND-MARKETING.md`,
`mast-backend/MARKETING-PLAYBOOK.md`, the CRM at the Worker's `/admin`.
